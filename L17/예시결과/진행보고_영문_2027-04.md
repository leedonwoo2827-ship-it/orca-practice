# Monthly Progress Report — April 2027

> Source: `L17_진행보고_한글.md` (practice material). All figures are fictitious. No financial information is included.

## 1. Key Progress This Month

- The inception report was submitted, and the Ministry of Education of Maleka completed its review.
- Instructional design was completed for 8 of the 40 e-learning courses. Of these, 3 have completed review and approval by local experts.
- Translation of the learning management system (LMS) interface into English was completed. Translation into Malekan will begin in May 2027.
- School lists were received for the Northern Province and the Central Province, two of the three pilot provinces. The list for the Coastal Province is to be confirmed (TBC).

## 2. Schedule Change

- Master Trainer Workshop: 10 to 14 May 2027 → 24 to 28 May 2027 (at the request of the National Teacher Training Institute (NTTI), owing to the examination schedule in the Northern Province)

## 3. Risks and Responses

| Risk | Impact | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Shortage of Malekan translation personnel | Delay in the review and approval of courses | Recruitment of 1 additional local expert under consideration |
| Delay in the school list for the Coastal Province | Delay in preparations for pilot operation | Request to the Ministry by official letter |

## 4. Plan for Next Month

- Delivery of the Master Trainer Workshop (60 participants)
- Instructional design for 6 additional e-learning courses
- Issuance of LMS test accounts and inspection of the server environment
